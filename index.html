The Complete Operation Order

1 · Understand the Patient

Read the story, age, temperament and risk. This tells you why the operation is needed.

2 · Build the Plan

Choose staff, instruments, anesthesia style and recovery destination. Recommended Plan does this for you.

3 · Stabilize

Connect monitoring and keep oxygen, blood pressure and temperature in safe game ranges.

4 · Prepare

Sterile preparation reduces contamination. The yellow marker shows the next required area.

5 · Perform the Repair

Select the named tool and follow the glowing guide. Each operation has its own sequence.

6 · Recover and Review

Move the patient to recovery, respond to needs, then review your detailed performance grade.
